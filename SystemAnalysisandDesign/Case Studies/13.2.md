Chain Link Consulting is an IT consulting firm specializing in system security issues. The company’s president has asked you to help make a presentation to potential clients at next month’s trade show meeting. First, considering all security levels, the president wants you to review system security issues. Then, you should devise a list of ways Chain Link could test a client’s security practices, getting a real-world vulnerability assessment.

To make matters more interesting, you have been told it is okay to be creative in your recommendations but not to propose any action that would be illegal or unethical. For example, it would be acceptable to pose as a job applicant with false references to see if they were being checked, but it would not be appropriate to pick a lock and enter the computer room.

Your report is due tomorrow. What will you suggest?
