North Hills College has decided to implement a new registration system allowing students to register online and in person. As an IT manager, you decide to set up a JAD session to help define the requirements for the new system. The North Hills organization is fairly typical, with an administrative staff including a registrar, student support and services team, business office, IT group, and several academic departments. Using this information, you start planning to carry out the JAD session. Whom would you invite to the session, and why? What would be your agenda for the session, and what would take place at each stage?

I would invite everyone on the admin staff as they are the ones in charge of overseeing all changes to any important system regarding the organization and should be privy on anything new in regards to the the systems that keep it running. The IT group should know how to use the system when it is implemented and should be able to train people in how to use and interact with it. Discussing the cost with the business office will be essential so that there is a value of time and dollar amount that can be dedicated on this project as well as its scale and complexity. The academic department should be informed of how to use such as system so that they can in turn tell the students how to register for classes and can be accomplished with the head of each department attending and training those below them until it reaches the students.
