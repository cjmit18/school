Very briefly discuss the difference between two file-oriented systems, and a  a DBMS.

A file-oriented system uses one or more files that aren't linked in any way making it so you would have to enter certain information twice or more based on how your system is setup and is more prone to errors due to this as compared to a DBMS which allows linkage between tables and thus allows you to enter information into a record and its immediately updated across the entire document.
