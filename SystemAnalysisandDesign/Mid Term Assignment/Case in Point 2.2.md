You are the IT director at Attaway Airlines, a small regional air carrier. You chair the company’s systems review committee and deal with strong disagreements about two key projects. The marketing manager says it is vital to have a new computerized reservation system that can provide better customer service and reduce operational costs. The vice president of finance is equally adamant that a new accounting system is needed immediately because it will be costly to adjust the current system to new federal reporting requirements. The VP outranks the marketing manager, and the VP is your boss. The next meeting is set for 9:00 a.m. tomorrow. How will you prepare for the vigorous discussions likely to occur at the meeting? What questions and issues should be raised?

I would prepare by researching and gathering the specific needs of both systems and compare and contrast them to see what is really important right now and how much it would effect the company if it is and if it isnt implemented. I would take into account the needs of each system such as if it is mandated by law if we need to add something to our systems by necessity or if what we are adding are niceties that would make our workflow better. I think asking questions as to why these systems need to be implemented, how much work would go into implementing them and how much it would cost for each project should be discussed as well as time and requirement constraints should be addressed so that that their is a clear picture on the needs of either system.
