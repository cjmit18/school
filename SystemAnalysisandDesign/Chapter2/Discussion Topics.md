1. One of your co-workers says, “Mission statements are nice, but they don’t change things down here where the work gets done.” How would you reply?
    
2. Discuss how a company’s financial status can affect systems projects.



I would say that a mission statement might not affect where the work gets done but they do effect the thought that goes into the work. Using Google as an example "Don't be evil" wont stop every single person in the company to abide by this vague moral compass but it will allow the people who do think its important to take this mission statement and apply it to the things that they do both in and out of work.

If the company is losing money or going through a difficult time they might delay projects or cancel them all together but if they are doing well they might create more projects to get done because they have the capital to expand out more.
