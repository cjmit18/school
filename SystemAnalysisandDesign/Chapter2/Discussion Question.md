
#### Case in Point 2.1. Pets for Rent

Pets for Rent is an innovative company that brings puppies to corporate meetings. The company’s CEO says that having puppies in a room with employees in an informal setting helps break down communication barriers and fosters a greater sense of teamwork and comradery. Since this is a new company, the CEO has asked you if a mission statement is necessary. After you review the chapter material, write a brief memo with your views. Be sure to include good (and not-so-good) examples of actual mission statements you find online.

I believe that a mission statement is essential, especially with startups and new companies, because it shows the consumer that your statement is positive, that you are a company that values and morals that you stand by and ultimately youre mission statment will become a part of youre brand and somemthing people will think about and remember, a good example of this is Googles mission statement or moto plastered on the front of the search engine was "Dont be evil" and has since disappeared and leaves us the consumer in abiguity of what theyre actual goal or moral compass lies. A good example of a bad company statement is Hershey's original statement: "Undisputed market dominance," which conveys nothing but corporate greed to consumers. A good one would be Google's old one, or Nike's "Just Do It," as it implies you can do anything you set your mind to.
