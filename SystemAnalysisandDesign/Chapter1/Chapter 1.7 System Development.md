**Structured Analysis** is a traditional system development technique that uses phases to plan, analyze, design, implement and support and information system. Processes and data are treated as separate components.

**Object-Oriented** Analysis is the act of understanding a system by identifying object that represent a real person, place, event or transaction. OO analysis is a popular approach that sees a system from the viewpoint of the objects as they function and interact with the system.

**Agile Methods** is a systems development method that attempts to develop a system incrementally by building a series of prototypes and constantly adjusting them to user requirements. related to adaptive methods

**Project Management** is the process of planning, scheduling, monitoring, controlling and reporting on the development of an information system
