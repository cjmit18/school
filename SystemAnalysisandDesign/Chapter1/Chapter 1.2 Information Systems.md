
***Hardware** consists of everything in the physical layer of the information system.
A **Data Center** is a concentration of networked computers working together.

**Moore's Law** Predicted that computing power would double every 18 - 24 months due to miniaturization of components 

---


**Software** refers to the programs that control the hardware and produce the desired information or results. - A program run by computers for a specific function or task. 

**Systems Software** Include programs that control a computer including the operating system, device drivers that communicate with the hardware and low-level utilities.

**Application Software** is a type of software program like email, a word processor or spreadsheet, used by employees in a typical office.

**Enterprise Applications** are company wide apps such as order processing or payroll systems.

A **horizontal system** is a basic system like an inventory or payroll package used by many companies.

A **vertical system** is a design used to meet unique requirements of a specific business or industry.

A **legacy system** is a system that is less advanced than currently used systems

---
**Big Data** are extremely large data sets (petabytes) requiring nontraditional approaches to deal with them. Sometimes  characterized by three terms: volume, variety and velocity.

**NoSQL databases** are databases that use a flat nontubular(nonrelational) structure to store and process large-scale data sets

---
**Processes** describe a procedure or task that users, managers, and IT staff perform. Also, the logical rules of a system that are applied to transform data into meaningful information. In data flow diagrams, a process receives input data and produces output with a different content, form or both

---

**Stakeholders** is anyone affected by a companies performance, such as customers, employees, suppliers, stockholders and community members

**Users** are stakeholders inside and outside the company who will interact with the system
