
**B2C** (Business-to-Consumer) is a commercial exchange between a business and a customer conducted over the internet.

**B2B** (Business to Business) is a commercial exchange between businesses typically enabled by the internet.

**Electronic Data Exchange (EDI)** is the process that involves the computer to computer transfer of data between companies.


**Supply chain management (SCM)** is the coordination, integration and management of materials, information and finances as the move from suppliers to customers both within and between companies. In an integrated supply chain, a customer order could cause a production planning system to schedule a work order triggering a call for certain parts from one or more suppliers.

**Supply Chain** is all of the companies that provide materials, services and functions to deliver products to a customer.

**C2C (Consumer to Consumer** is a commercial exchange between two consumers conducted over the internet.
