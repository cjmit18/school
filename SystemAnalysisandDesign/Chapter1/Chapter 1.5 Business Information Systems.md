
**Enterprise Computing** is an information system that supports company wide data management requirements such as airline reservations or credit card billing systems.

**Enterprise Resource Planning** is a process that establishes an enterprise wide strategy for IT resources. **ERP** defines a specific architecture, including data, processing, network and user interface design standards 

**Transaction Processing Systems** Operational Systems used to process day to day recurring business transactions such as customer billing

**Business Support Systems** provide job related information support to users at all company levels.

**Management Information Systems** is a computer based information system used in business planning, control, decision making and problem solving

**Radio Frequency Identification** is a tech that uses high frequency radio waves to track physical objects

A **knowledge base** is a popular systems development technique that uses a group of users, managers and IT professionals working together to gather information and discuss business needs and define new requirements 

**Inference Rules** Instructions that direct a knowledge management system to identify data patterns and relationships

**User Productivity Systems** are applications that provide employees of all levels a wide array of tools to improve job performance. (Word, email, excel)

**Groupware** applications that allow working in teams like slack, teams, chime

**Personal Digital Assistant** A program that responds to user requests through a nature interface such as regular speech to aid general purpose queries often embedded in devices such as  internet connected speakers and smartphones 

**Artificial Intelligence** is the attempt to re-create natural intelligence through software in machines

**Machine learning** Is an application of computer science and AI that uses automated approaches to pattern recognition and predictive analytics based on large data
