
 **Information technology** is a combination of hardware, software and telecommunication systems that support business operations, improve productivity, and help manager make decisions. It affects society and numerous different ways such as healthcare, the banking industry , finances and overall personal visibility.

**System analysis and design** is the process of developing information systems effectively use hardware, software, data, processes and people to support a company's business objectives.

**Information Systems** are a combination of information tech, people and data to support business requirements. The 5 key components are: hardware, software, data, processes, and people.

**System analyst** is someone who plans, analyzes and implements an information system. 

---
A **system** is a set of related components that produces specific results

A **mission critical system** is one that is vital to company operations

**Data** is the raw material or basic fact used by the information system.
**Information** is data that has been transformed into valuable output for users
